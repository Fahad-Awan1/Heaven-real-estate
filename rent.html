Frequently asked

Can’t see your question? or contact an advisor.

What do I need to apply?

Your CNIC (or passport for foreign nationals), recent salary slips or bank statements, and a reference. We also handle tenant police registration, required in Punjab and Islamabad.

Who pays the utility bills?

Usually the tenant pays electricity, gas and water. Ask about solar or a generator for load-shedding, and check meter readings on the day you move in.

How much do I pay upfront?

Typically two months’ security deposit, one month’s rent in advance and one month’s rent as commission. The deposit is returned at the end of the agreement.

Can I rent furnished?

Yes. Some homes come furnished or semi-furnished; ask your agent or Halo, and we’ll list exactly what stays in the agreement.
