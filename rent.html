Good to know

Frequently asked

Can’t see your question? or contact an advisor.

What do I need to apply?

Photo ID, recent payslips or bank statements, references and a credit check. Most tenants earn around three times the monthly rent.

Are pets allowed?

Many of our homes are pet friendly; look for the “Pet friendly” tag or ask Halo. A small pet deposit is usually required.

Is there a holding deposit?

Yes, typically one week’s rent, which is deducted from your first month once the lease is signed.

Can I rent furnished?

Yes. Several homes, such as the White Pavilion, come fully furnished with flexible lease options.
