Quick answers

When are you open?

Our offices are open Monday to Saturday, 9am – 7pm. Sunday viewings are available by appointment, and Halo answers 24/7.

Yes. Whether you’re buying, selling or renting, your first consultation and valuation are always free and without obligation.

Our core is LA County and Orange County’s coast, but our partner network covers most of California.

Can I visit without an appointment?
