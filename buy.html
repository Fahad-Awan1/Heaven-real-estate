Frequently asked

Can’t see your question? or contact an advisor.

Do buyers pay a fee to work with Sitara?

Not in our own projects. On resale homes our commission is typically 1%, and we’ll always confirm it in writing before you view.

How much do I need upfront?

Token money is often around 10% when the agreement is signed. For bank home finance, expect a 15–30% down payment. The home finance calculator on each listing shows an indicative monthly figure.

Can you help me buy from abroad?

Yes. Many of our buyers are overseas Pakistanis. We run live video tours, help with a power of attorney and can work with a Roshan Digital Account.

What are off-market homes?

Homes whose owners prefer a discreet sale. Roughly a third of our sales never appear on public portals.
